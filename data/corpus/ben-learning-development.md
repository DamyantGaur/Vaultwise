# Learning and Development Policy

Owner: Human Resources - Talent | Version 1.6

## Annual Learning Budget
Each employee has a learning budget of INR 40,000 per financial year for courses, books, conferences and online learning subscriptions. Unused budget does not roll over to the next financial year.

## Certifications
Fees for professional certification exams related to the role of the employee are reimbursed after the employee passes the exam. One retake is reimbursed if the first attempt is unsuccessful.

## Sponsored Programmes and Retention
For company-sponsored programmes costing more than INR 1,00,000, the employee signs a retention agreement. If the employee resigns within 12 months of completing the programme, the cost is recovered on a pro-rata basis.

## Learning Time
Employees may use up to 4 hours per month of working time for approved learning activities, recorded in PeopleHub.

## Approvals
Learning requests are raised in PeopleHub and approved by the reporting manager. Requests above INR 40,000 also need approval from the HR Business Partner.
