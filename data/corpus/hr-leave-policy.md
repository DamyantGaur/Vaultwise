# Leave Policy

Owner: Human Resources | Applies to: All full-time employees in India | Version 4.2

## Annual (Earned) Leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at the rate of 2 days per completed month of service. Employees on probation accrue leave but may use at most 6 days of annual leave before confirmation.

## Carry Forward and Encashment
A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December. Annual leave is not encashed during employment; at the time of exit, up to 30 accumulated days are encashed at the basic salary rate.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave to extend a vacation and cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive days.

## Applying for Leave
All leave must be applied for in the PeopleHub HR portal. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be applied for at least 7 calendar days in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Unplanned leave must be informed to the manager by 10:00 AM on the day of absence.

## Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year. The holiday list is published in PeopleHub every December for the following year. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive one compensatory day off, which must be used within 60 days.
