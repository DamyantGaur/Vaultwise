# Multi-Factor Authentication and Access Control Standard

Owner: IT Security | Version 2.1

## MFA Requirement
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for every employee and contractor account, including email, VPN, HR and finance systems. The VeriAuth authenticator app with push approval is the preferred second factor. Hardware security keys are mandatory for administrators. SMS one-time codes are not permitted for administrator accounts.

## Lost or New Phone
If you change or lose the phone with your authenticator app, raise a ticket with the IT Helpdesk to re-register MFA. Identity is verified on a video call with your manager present before MFA is reset.

## Least Privilege
Access to systems is granted on the principle of least privilege, based on job role. Access requests are raised in the ServiceHub portal and approved by the data owner.

## Access Reviews
Managers review the access of their team members every quarter. Access that is not re-approved during the quarterly review is revoked automatically.

## Privileged Access
Administrative access to production systems is granted just-in-time through the Privileged Access Manager for a maximum of 8 hours per request. All privileged sessions are recorded.

## Leavers
All access for an exiting employee is revoked by 6:00 PM on their last working day.
