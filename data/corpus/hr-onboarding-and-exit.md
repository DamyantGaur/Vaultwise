# Onboarding, Probation and Exit Policy

Owner: Human Resources | Version 1.8

## First Day
New joiners receive their laptop and access badge on day 1 and are assigned an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 90 days. Mandatory induction sessions on security and code of conduct must be completed within the first 2 weeks.

## Probation
All new employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The manager may extend probation once, by up to 3 months. Confirmation is communicated in writing through PeopleHub.

## Notice Period
During probation, the notice period on either side is 15 days. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for individual contributors and 90 days for managers and above. Notice period buy-out is allowed only with the approval of the department head.

## Exit Process
Resigning employees must submit their resignation in PeopleHub. On the last working day, all company assets, including laptops and access badges, must be returned to IT. The full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day, and the relieving letter is issued after asset clearance.

## Rehire
Former employees who left in good standing are eligible for rehire after 6 months.
