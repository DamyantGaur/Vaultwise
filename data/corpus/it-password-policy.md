# Password and Credential Policy

Owner: IT Security | Version 3.2

## Password Requirements
Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged. Passwords must not reuse any of the last 5 passwords and must not contain the employee's name or the word Veridian.

## Expiry
Passwords do not expire on a fixed schedule. A password must be changed immediately if there is any suspicion that it has been exposed, or when IT Security forces a reset after an incident.

## Account Lockout
An account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts. Repeated lockouts are investigated by the Security Operations Centre.

## Password Manager
Employees must store work credentials only in VaultKey, the company-approved password manager. Storing passwords in browsers, spreadsheets, sticky notes or chat messages is prohibited.

## Shared and Service Accounts
Shared accounts are not permitted for individual use. Service account credentials are stored in the Secrets Vault and rotated every 90 days by the owning team.

## Resetting a Password
Forgotten passwords can be reset through the self-service portal at reset.veridian.internal after verifying with multi-factor authentication. If self-service fails, contact the IT Helpdesk.
