# Performance Management Policy

Owner: Human Resources | Version 2.3

## Review Cycles
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year: a mid-year review in October and an annual review in April. Goals are set in the PeopleHub Performance module within 30 days of the start of each cycle.

## Rating Scale
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Does not meet expectations), 2 (Partially meets), 3 (Meets expectations), 4 (Exceeds expectations) and 5 (Outstanding). Ratings are calibrated by a panel of department leaders.

## Promotions
Promotions are processed once a year, effective 1 April. To be eligible, an employee must have spent at least 18 months in the current role and received a rating of 4 or higher in two consecutive review cycles.

## Performance Improvement Plan
An employee rated 1 is placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 days, with fortnightly check-ins with the manager and HR. Failure to meet PIP goals may result in separation.

## Salary Revision
Annual salary revisions are communicated in June and are effective from 1 April of the same year, linked to the annual review rating.
