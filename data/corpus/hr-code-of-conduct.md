# Code of Business Conduct and Ethics

Owner: Legal and Compliance | Version 5.0

## Gifts and Hospitality
Employees may accept gifts from customers, vendors or partners only if the value is below INR 5,000 per gift. Gifts above this value must be politely declined or handed over to the Compliance team. Cash or cash equivalents, such as gift cards, must never be accepted regardless of value.

## Conflicts of Interest
Any actual or potential conflict of interest, including a family relationship with a vendor or a second job, must be disclosed in the Ethics Portal within 14 days of becoming aware of it. Outside employment requires prior written approval from HR.

## Reporting Concerns
Concerns about misconduct, fraud or harassment can be reported to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline at 1800-200-4455, which is available 24 hours a day. Reports are acknowledged within 3 business days.

## Non-Retaliation
Veridian Systems strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Retaliation is treated as a serious violation and can lead to termination.

## Prevention of Sexual Harassment
Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act. A complaint should be filed within 3 months of the incident and the inquiry is completed within 90 days.

## Annual Certification
All employees must complete the Code of Conduct training and certification every year by 31 March.
