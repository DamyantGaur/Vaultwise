# Health Insurance and Wellness Benefits

Owner: Human Resources - Total Rewards | Version 2024.1

## Group Medical Insurance
All employees are covered under a group medical insurance policy with a family floater sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per year. The policy covers the employee, spouse or partner, and up to 2 children. Pre-existing conditions are covered from day one.

## Parents Cover
Employees can add parents or parents-in-law to an optional top-up policy with a sum insured of INR 3,00,000. The premium for parents cover is paid by the employee through payroll deduction.

## Enrolment
Dependants must be enrolled within 30 days of joining or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child. Outside these windows, changes can only be made at annual renewal in January.

## Cashless Claims
Cashless hospitalisation is available at network hospitals through the insurer's TPA app. For planned hospitalisation, pre-authorisation must be requested at least 48 hours in advance.

## Wellness Allowance
Employees receive a wellness allowance of INR 12,000 per year that can be claimed for gym memberships, sports equipment, yoga classes or mental health apps. Unused wellness allowance does not carry forward.

## Employee Assistance Programme
Free and confidential counselling sessions are available to employees and their family members through the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), up to 6 sessions per person per year.
