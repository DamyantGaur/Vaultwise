# Acceptable Use of Generative AI Tools

Owner: Information Security and Legal | Version 1.3

## Approved Tools
Only generative AI tools listed in the AI Tools Register on the intranet may be used for work. Using an unapproved chatbot or AI tool with company information is a policy violation.

## Data Restrictions
Confidential and Restricted information must never be entered into public generative AI tools. Internal information may be used only with tools marked "enterprise" in the AI Tools Register, which do not retain prompts for training.

## Human Review
All AI-generated content must be reviewed by a person before it is sent to customers or published. AI-generated source code must go through the standard code review and security scanning process before it is merged.

## Requesting a New Tool
Teams can request evaluation of a new AI tool through ServiceHub. The AI Governance Board reviews requests within 15 business days, covering security, privacy and licensing.

## Transparency
Customer-facing content that is substantially generated by AI must be disclosed as such where required by contract or law.
