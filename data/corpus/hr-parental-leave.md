# Parental Leave Policy

Owner: Human Resources | Applies to: All employees regardless of gender | Version 2.0

## Maternity Leave
Birthing parents are entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for the first two children, of which up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date. For a third or subsequent child, maternity leave is 12 weeks.

## Paternity and Secondary Caregiver Leave
Non-birthing parents are entitled to 15 working days of fully paid paternity leave. Paternity leave must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption of the child and may be split into at most two blocks.

## Adoption Leave
Employees who legally adopt a child below the age of three months receive 12 weeks of paid adoption leave from the date the child is handed over. The adoption deed must be uploaded to PeopleHub.

## Return to Work
After maternity or adoption leave, employees may opt for a flexible return period of 3 months working a reduced 30-hour week at full pay. A creche reimbursement of up to INR 8,000 per month is available until the child turns 3 years old.

## Eligibility
Parental leave is available from the first day of employment; there is no minimum service requirement. Leave must be requested in PeopleHub at least 8 weeks before the expected start date, except in emergencies.
