# Security Incident Reporting and Response Policy

Owner: Security Operations Centre (SOC) | Version 3.4

## What to Report
Report any suspected security incident immediately, including phishing emails, malware, unauthorised access, lost devices and accidental sharing of confidential data.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails must be reported using the Report Phish button in the mail client. Do not click links, open attachments or forward the email to colleagues. If you entered your password on a suspicious page, reset it immediately and inform the SOC.

## Severity Levels and Response Times
Incidents are classified into four severity levels. SEV1 (critical, such as active data breach or ransomware) receives a response within 15 minutes. SEV2 (high) receives a response within 1 hour. SEV3 (medium) receives a response within 4 hours. SEV4 (low) receives a response within 1 business day.

## Contacting the SOC
The SOC is reachable 24x7 at soc@veridian.example or on the SOC hotline at extension 7777.

## Personal Data Breaches
Any incident involving personal data must be escalated to the Data Protection Officer (DPO) within 6 hours of discovery so that regulatory notification obligations, including reporting to CERT-In, can be met.

## Post-Incident Review
A post-incident review is completed within 10 business days for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident, and its corrective actions are tracked to closure.
