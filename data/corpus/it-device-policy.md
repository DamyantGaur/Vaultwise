# End-User Device Policy

Owner: IT Operations | Version 4.0

## Company Laptops
Every employee is issued a company laptop managed by IT. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years, or earlier if a hardware failure cannot be repaired. Full-disk encryption, endpoint protection and automatic updates must stay enabled at all times.

## Software Installation
Only software from the Company Portal app catalogue may be installed. Requests for software that is not in the catalogue are raised in ServiceHub and reviewed by IT within 5 business days.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
A lost or stolen laptop or phone containing company data must be reported to IT Security within 1 hour by calling the IT Helpdesk at extension 4357 or emailing security@veridian.example. For theft, a police report must also be filed within 48 hours.

## Personal Devices (BYOD)
Personal phones and tablets may be used only for email, calendar and chat, and only after enrolling in the company mobile device management (MDM) app. Company files must not be downloaded to personal laptops.

## Removable Media
USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Exceptions require approval from IT Security and are valid for a maximum of 30 days.

## Returning Devices
Devices must be returned to IT on the last working day. Unreturned devices are recovered from the full and final settlement at the depreciated value.
