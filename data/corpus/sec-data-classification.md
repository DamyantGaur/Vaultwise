# Data Classification and Handling Policy

Owner: Information Security and Legal | Version 2.2

## Classification Levels
All company information is classified into one of four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. The data owner assigns the classification when the information is created.

## Examples
Public: published marketing material and press releases. Internal: org charts, internal announcements and most policies. Confidential: customer contracts, pricing, source code and financial forecasts. Restricted: customer personal data, employee salary and health records, authentication secrets and encryption keys.

## Handling Rules
Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Restricted data may be shared externally only with approval from the data owner and Legal, through an approved secure file transfer service. Documents must carry a classification label in the header.

## Retention
Customer contract records are retained for 8 years after contract end. Employee records are retained for 7 years after exit. Data past its retention period is securely deleted.

## Disposal
Paper documents classified Confidential or Restricted must be placed in locked shredding bins. Storage media are destroyed by IT through a certified vendor.
