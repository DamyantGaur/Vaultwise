# VPN and Remote Access Guide

Owner: IT Networks | Version 2.6

## When to Use the VPN
The SecureLink VPN is required to access internal applications, file shares and code repositories when you are not connected to the office network. Email, chat and PeopleHub are available without VPN.

## Connecting
Open the SecureLink client, choose the nearest gateway (Bengaluru, Pune or Frankfurt) and sign in with your company credentials and MFA. Split tunnelling is disabled, so all traffic goes through the VPN while connected.

## Session Limits
VPN sessions disconnect automatically after 12 hours, and after 30 minutes of inactivity. Reconnect by signing in again.

## Troubleshooting
If you cannot connect: first check your internet connection, then restart the SecureLink client, then switch to a different gateway. If the problem continues, raise a ticket in ServiceHub or call the IT Helpdesk at extension 4357. The IT Helpdesk is available 24x7 for VPN issues.

## Public Wi-Fi
When working from public Wi-Fi such as cafes or airports, connect to the VPN before opening any company application.
