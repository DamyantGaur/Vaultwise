# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

Owner: Human Resources and Workplace | Version 3.1

## Hybrid Work Model
Veridian Systems follows a hybrid model. Employees are expected to work from their assigned office at least 3 days per week. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are core collaboration days on which teams should plan to be in the office.

## Fully Remote Arrangements
A fully remote arrangement requires written approval from the department Vice President and HR. Fully remote approvals are reviewed every 12 months.

## Home Office Support
Employees on the hybrid or remote model receive a one-time home office stipend of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. In addition, an internet reimbursement of up to INR 1,500 per month is paid through payroll on submission of the broadband bill in PeopleHub.

## Working From Another Country
Employees may work from outside India for a maximum of 30 days per calendar year. This requires prior approval from HR and the Legal team at least 21 days before travel, because of tax and immigration implications.

## Working Hours
Core availability hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, during which employees must be reachable on chat. Employees should update their calendar with their work location every week.
