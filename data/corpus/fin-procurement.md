# Procurement and Vendor Management Policy

Owner: Finance - Procurement | Version 3.0

## Purchase Requisition
All purchases of goods or services for the company must start with a purchase requisition raised in the ProcureNow portal. A purchase order (PO) must be issued before the vendor delivers goods or raises an invoice. Invoices without a valid PO are returned to the vendor.

## Competitive Quotations
Purchases above INR 2,00,000 require at least 3 competitive quotations. Purchases above INR 25,00,000 require a formal tender evaluated by a committee including Finance and the requesting department.

## Vendor Onboarding
New vendors are onboarded through ProcureNow after submitting their PAN, GST registration, cancelled cheque and a signed vendor code of conduct. Vendors handling company data must also pass an information security assessment by the Security team.

## Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are net 45 days from the date of a valid invoice. Micro and small enterprises registered under MSME are paid within 30 days.

## Software Purchases
Any software or SaaS subscription, including free tools that require company data, must be reviewed by IT and Security before purchase.
