# Business Travel Policy

Owner: Finance | Version 6.0

## Booking
All business travel must be booked through the corporate TravelDesk portal at least 14 days before departure for international trips and 7 days before departure for domestic trips. Bookings made outside TravelDesk are not reimbursed unless TravelDesk was unavailable.

## Class of Travel
Domestic flights are booked in economy class for all employees. For international flights longer than 6 hours, Directors and above may travel in business class; all other employees travel in premium economy where available. Train travel in AC 2-tier is permitted for domestic trips under 500 km.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at INR 7,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and INR 4,500 per night in other Indian cities. International hotels are booked by TravelDesk from the approved list.

## Per Diem
A daily allowance (per diem) covers meals and local incidentals: INR 2,500 per day for domestic travel and USD 75 per day for international travel. Per diem is not paid for days on which meals are provided by the client or event organiser.

## Travel Approval
Domestic travel requires reporting manager approval. International travel requires approval from the department Vice President. Travel insurance is provided automatically for all international trips booked through TravelDesk.
