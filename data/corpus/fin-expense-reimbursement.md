# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Owner: Finance | Version 4.4

## Submission
Business expenses must be submitted in the SpendWise expense portal within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days require approval from the Finance Controller and claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Receipts
An itemised receipt or tax invoice is required for every expense above INR 500. For expenses of INR 500 or less, a self-declaration in SpendWise is sufficient.

## Approvals
Expense claims are approved by the reporting manager. Any single claim above INR 50,000 additionally requires approval from the Finance Controller.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed to the salary bank account of the employee within 10 business days of final approval.

## Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment are capped at INR 3,000 per person, and the names of attendees and the business purpose must be recorded in the claim. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client events.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Traffic fines, personal entertainment, spa services, laundry for trips shorter than 4 days and loss of personal belongings are not reimbursable.
