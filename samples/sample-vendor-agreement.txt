MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is made between Orbitline Technologies Private Limited ("the Client") and Tessaract Infra Services Private Limited ("the Vendor"). This is a fictional sample document for testing Vaultwise.

1. Scope of Services
The Vendor will provide managed database hosting, monitoring and 24x7 technical support for the Client's analytics platform, as described in Schedule A.

2. Term and Renewal
This Agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for 3 years. It renews automatically for further periods of 1 year unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current term.

3. Fees and Payment
The Client will pay a fixed fee of INR 6,50,000 per month. The Vendor invoices monthly in arrears, and the Client pays each valid invoice within 30 days of receipt. Late payments carry interest of 1.5 percent per month.

4. Service Levels
The Vendor guarantees 99.9 percent monthly availability of the hosted platform. Priority 1 incidents must receive a response within 30 minutes and be resolved within 4 hours. For each 0.1 percent of availability below the target, the Client receives a service credit of 5 percent of the monthly fee, capped at 25 percent of that month's fee.

5. Data Protection
All Client data must be stored and processed only in data centres located in India. The Vendor must notify the Client of any personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware of it. The Vendor may not use subcontractors to process Client data without the Client's prior written consent.

6. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other party's confidential information secret during the Agreement and for 5 years after it ends.

7. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid by the Client in the 12 months before the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or data protection obligations.

8. Termination
The Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 90 days written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days of written notice.

9. Exit and Data Return
On termination, the Vendor will return all Client data within 15 days and securely delete all remaining copies within 30 days, providing a written certificate of deletion.

10. Governing Law and Disputes
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes will be resolved by arbitration in Mumbai before a sole arbitrator.
